Loch

Your wallet answers here.

Loch only asks.

The Loch extension opened this page because a wallet can only be asked from a web page. Keep it open until Loch says it is done; it closes by itself. Answer your wallet in the wallet’s own window.

What your wallet will ask
To connect, and then to sign one message: “Loch key derivation v1”. The first time it asks for that signature twice. When you dive in, it asks you to approve the deposit.
What the signature is for
It derives the keys of your shielded balance, on your device. It moves nothing and costs nothing.
What to never do
Never sign “Loch key derivation v1” on any other website. Whoever has that signature can read and spend your shielded balance. Loch asks for it only on this page, and only after you pressed Connect wallet or Unlock in the Loch extension.
What this page does
Nothing by itself. It runs no script, loads nothing from other sites and keeps nothing.
